# Linear A method parity and rights frontier

Phrygian is driven toward the same evidence-control standard as Linear A, while exploiting the stronger linguistic state of a deciphered alphabetic language.

## Established machine layer
- licensed UD reference snapshot: 203 sentences, 1,921 tokens, 162 TM source labels;
- canonical epigraphic layer remains sealed at zero until source-level admission is justified;
- TM discovery benchmark and TITUS heading catalogue are maintained as discovery/counting views, not certified physical totals;
- explicit source-dependence graph, disagreement register and rights/source matrix;
- rights-allowlisted offline browser;
- read-only research API;
- loss-aware canonical/reference exports;
- reproducible audits and pre-expert parity validation;
- expert-review queue and source reconciliation dossiers.

## Counting and independence
536 TM attestations are a discovery benchmark, not a certified exhaustive world total. 305 TITUS headings are catalogue headings, not inscription totals. 162 UD TM labels and 161 numeric stems are reference identity views, not independently reconciled physical objects.

UD, TITUS, TM and the editions they cite must never be multiplied into independent witnesses when they share lineage.

## Critical rights/access frontier
Systematic critical-edition collation cannot be completed from the present redistributable layer without establishing lawful structured-use/redistribution rights for the foundational Brixhe–Lejeune corpus, Obrador-Cursach's modern catalogue/edition, and TITUS source text. Item-level visual evidence may carry separate restrictions; the Anatolian Alphabetic Database explicitly withholds some photographs and letter cutouts for lack of publication permission.

This repository therefore retains bibliographic facts, source lineage, lawful licensed reference data, short attributed evidence where permitted, hashes/locators and original audits rather than reconstructing protected editions through dependent copies.

Expert adjudication of readings, restorations, dating and disputed metadata remains downstream scientific review, not unfinished software engineering.
